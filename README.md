# Kansas City Employee Ownership

A public information site on employee ownership as a business succession option for Kansas
City business owners, with a structured intake that helps an owner understand whether their
company is a plausible fit and what issues it would face.

Built by Project Team 5 in Urban Innovation (LAW 8925), UMKC School of Law, Fall 2026 —
Anthony Ross, Camron Zerr, Mandy Lawson, and Kirby Garrett, advised by Prof. Evan Absher.

**This site provides general information. It is not legal advice and does not create an
attorney-client relationship.**
