# Team task list

This is how we know who is doing what. It is the only place work in progress is visible to
everyone else — while you are writing, your changes are private to your copy until you say
your section is ready.

You do not maintain this by hand. Tell Claude what you are picking up and it records it
immediately, before you start, and it keeps checking this file throughout your session. If
someone else claims something while you are working, you will hear about it then rather than
a week later.

Ask *"what's everyone working on?"* any time. Or *"what should I pick up?"*

**Every item has a "Covers" line.** That is the important part. It says what territory the
item owns, so work that *sounds* different but *is* the same gets caught before two people
spend a week on it. Read the Covers lines, not the titles.

Status is `Unassigned`, `In progress`, or `Finished`.

Not everything here will get built. It is a map of the work, not a promise. Better to see
the whole thing and choose than to discover it in November.

**Covers:** what a visitor sees first — what this site is, who it is for, and where to go

editing.

### ESOP explainer
`Unassigned` · Camron's section
**Covers:** everything answering "what is an ESOP" and "how does one work" — the trust,

`Unassigned` · Kirby's section
**Covers:** employee ownership trusts and worker cooperatives — what each is, how each

explainers.

### When employee ownership is the wrong answer
`Unassigned`
**Covers:** the honest counterweight. Situations where an ESOP or a co-op is a bad fit,
over-leveraged deals, the repurchase obligation, employees holding undiversified retirement

### What the process looks like
`Unassigned`
**Covers:** the sequence and timeline of an actual transaction, from an owner first
