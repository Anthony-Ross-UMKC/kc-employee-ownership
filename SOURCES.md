## What counts as verified

- **A primary source.** The statute itself, the agency's own page, the study, the
  organization's own published figure. Not an article describing one.
- **Recorded precisely.** Enough detail that someone else could find it again: title,
  publisher, date, and a link or citation.
- **Matching what we would say.** If the source says something narrower than the claim, the
  claim gets narrowed. Do not stretch a source to cover a bigger statement.

If a figure turns out to be older than it looked, or to say something different, that is
worth knowing now rather than in December.
